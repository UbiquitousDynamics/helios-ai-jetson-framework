# Team Notice #22/2025 – Control Stop Operating Times

This notice was issued on July 25, 2025 by World Solar Challenge Mission Control.  
It outlines the official opening and closing times of all control stops along the route.  
Teams must strictly adhere to these times as defined in the Route Notes.  
Failure to comply may result in penalties or disqualification.

The first control stop is in Katherine.  
It will be open on Sunday, August 24, 2025, from 11:00 to 17:00.  

The second stop is in Dunmarra.  
It opens on Sunday, August 24 at 15:00 and closes on Monday, August 25 at 14:00.  

Tennant Creek is the next stop.  
It opens on Monday, August 25 at 10:00 and closes on Tuesday, August 26 at 11:00.  

Barrow Creek will be open from Monday, August 25 at 13:00 until Tuesday, August 26 at 15:00.  

Alice Springs opens on Tuesday, August 26 at 08:00 and closes on Wednesday, August 27 at 10:30.  

The Erldunda stop is available from Tuesday, August 26 at 11:00 to Wednesday, August 27 at 14:00.  

Coober Pedy opens on Tuesday, August 26 at 16:00 and will close on Thursday, August 28 at 13:00.  

The Glendambo control stop operates from Wednesday, August 27 at 10:00 until Thursday, August 28 at 17:00.  

Port Augusta opens on Wednesday, August 27 at 14:00 and closes on Friday, August 29 at 13:00.  

Victoria Park is the final control stop.  
It will be open from Thursday, August 28 at 08:00 until Friday, August 29 at 17:30.  

Teams arriving on Saturday by trailer will be staged into Victoria Square.  
The order of arrival will be managed and confirmed by Mission Control.

If you have any questions about this schedule or other logistics, you can contact the organization via email at teams@worldsolarchallenge.org.
